Vedic Maths: Practice Makes Perfect

An app by Learn Logic

Learn 16 quick mental maths techniques from Vedic Maths — from checking answers with digit sums to squaring numbers, dividing and solving equations in your head.

Each technique has an animated worked example, practice questions with hints, and a timed test. Pass a test to unlock the next one and earn up to three stars. A new Daily Challenge every day keeps your streak going.

Free, with occasional adverts. No account needed — your progress stays on your device.
